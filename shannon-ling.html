Biography

A heart for animals, a passion for people, and a commitment to making a difference—Shannon Ling brings all three to Women Powering Change Florida. Involved since the organization’s beginning, Shannon is a dedicated member of the team whose compassion and community spirit reflect its mission of bringing people together for meaningful change.

As Outreach & Volunteer Coordinator and Secretary, Shannon brings a welcoming presence and a service-minded approach to her work. She values personal connections and believes everyone has something meaningful to contribute.
