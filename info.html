Women Powering Change Florida

Choose Purpose. Lift Others. Create Impact.

We bring women, businesses, and community together to turn shared talents and resources into meaningful change.

Who we are

A collective of women empowering each other to drive positive social, economic, and cultural change in our communities.

We believe that when women bring their businesses, talents, relationships, and resources together, we can create meaningful change that reaches far beyond one organization or one event.

One local cause. Every quarter.

Each quarter we choose a local charity or community effort and put our collective energy behind it.

  • WomenLifting up women and the families who depend on them.
  • AnimalsRescues, shelters, and sanctuaries giving animals a second chance.
  • ChildrenNourishing, educating, and encouraging the next generation.
  • Disaster ReliefLocal relief when neighbors need it most.

How it works

  1. Listen to our community. We find local needs and organizations doing meaningful work where support makes a real difference.
  2. Put our talents to work. Members use their businesses, networks, skills, resources, and voices to move the cause forward.
  3. Make an impact together. We rally women, business owners, sponsors, volunteers, and partners to create visible change.

Meet our founder

Terra Tominelli, Founder & President. Entrepreneur, educator, and community advocate in Sarasota–Manatee.

Read Terra's story

Terra Tominelli is a lifelong entrepreneur, educational innovator, and community advocate whose career spans more than 25 years in education and eight years in fashion. As Founder and President of Women Powering Change Florida and the founder of two women-owned businesses, she brings an enduring commitment to creative leadership, purposeful collaboration, and community service.

As founder of TerraNichol Academy of The Arts, Terra established the first school of its kind in Sarasota County, combining a Reggio Emilia approach with Montessori-inspired principles on a certified eco-friendly campus. TerraNichol Academy was voted Best School in Sarasota County, and Terra received the Sierra Club Citizen of the Year award for its sustainability program. She has also been nominated for the Good Hero Award and SRQ Magazine’s Women Who Roar.

Through Pink Sebastian Boutique, Terra extends her creative leadership into personal styling, brand collaborations, and charity fashion shows. At Women Powering Change Florida, she brings local businesses, nonprofit partners, and community members together to empower women and support meaningful causes.
