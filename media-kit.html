About Us

Short description

Women Powering Change Florida brings women, businesses, and community together to turn shared talents and resources into meaningful change, supporting one local cause every quarter.

Full description

Women Powering Change Florida is a collective of women empowering each other to drive positive social, economic, and cultural change in our communities. We unite women of all backgrounds, women-owned businesses, and local partners to support one local cause each quarter across four focus areas: women, animals, children, and disaster relief.
