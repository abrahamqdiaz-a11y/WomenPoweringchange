Biography

Terra Tominelli is a lifelong entrepreneur, educational innovator, and community advocate whose career spans more than 25 years in education and eight years in fashion. As Founder and President of Women Powering Change Florida and the founder of two women-owned businesses, she brings an enduring commitment to creative leadership, purposeful collaboration, and community service.

As founder of TerraNichol Academy of The Arts, Terra established the first school of its kind in Sarasota County, combining a Reggio Emilia approach with Montessori-inspired principles on a certified eco-friendly campus. Working with children from preschool through elementary school, including learners as young as 18 months, she developed an eclectic, hands-on, play-based nature school curriculum that encouraged curiosity, creative expression, and environmental responsibility.

The school’s visual and performing arts program encompassed art appreciation, performing arts, culinary arts, and photography. Spanish and Italian enriched language learning, while Zumba and yoga introduced movement and fitness. Gardening, a waste-free organic lunch program, and a toxin-free learning environment made sustainability part of daily school life. This integrated approach connected artistic exploration, wellness, and care for the natural world.

TerraNichol Academy was voted Best School in Sarasota County. Terra received the Sierra Club Citizen of the Year award for her sustainability program and its environmental impact with students. Her community and philanthropic contributions have also earned nominations for the Good Hero Award and SRQ Magazine’s Women Who Roar.

Through Pink Sebastian Boutique, Terra extends her creative leadership into personal styling, brand collaborations, and charity fashion shows. At Women Powering Change Florida, she brings local businesses, nonprofit partners, and community members together to empower women and support meaningful causes. Her leadership reflects a belief that shared talents and strong relationships can create lasting community impact.
