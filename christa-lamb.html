Biography

Christa Lamb is a Sarasota-based entrepreneur, published children’s book author, and community advocate with a background in business management, education, and leadership. She is the founder and owner of Wiggle Your Tail Pet Care and co-owner of Lamb Insurance Agency, as well as a licensed Property & Casualty Insurance Agent and notary public.

Before moving to Florida, Christa served as a Director of Operations in Los Angeles, building a strong foundation in business management and leadership. She also worked as a substitute teacher for the Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD) and authored the children’s book Cleaning with Mama.

Under her leadership, Wiggle Your Tail Pet Care earned Platinum in the 2025 Best of SRQ Local awards, Gold in 2026, and a 2026 Best of Florida Honorable Mention. The company was also featured in Suncoast Pet Magazine in 2022 and 2026.

Passionate about animal welfare and community involvement, Christa supports local rescues, fundraising initiatives, and charitable partnerships. As Vice President of Women Powering Change Florida, she is committed to supporting women, connecting local businesses, and bringing people together to make a meaningful difference in the community.
