Our Mission

Empowering women to create positive social, economic, and cultural change.

Women Powering Change is a collective of women empowering each other to drive positive social, economic, and cultural change in our communities. We unite women of all backgrounds to create opportunities for personal growth, collaboration, and connection.

Our Philosophy

Choose Purpose. Lift Others. Create Impact.

We believe that when women bring their businesses, talents, relationships, and resources together, we can create meaningful change that reaches far beyond one organization or one event.

One Quarter. One Local Cause. One Community Moving Forward.

Each quarter, Women Powering Change Florida selects a local charity or community effort to uplift. Our focus is intentionally broad because our community's needs are broad: women, animals, children, and local disaster or emergency relief efforts. We bring our collective energy, connections, unique talents, businesses, and resources together to shine a light on a cause that needs support.

We don't believe one person has to do everything. We believe every woman has something valuable to give — a skill, a connection, a voice, a product, a service, a donation, a platform, or simply a willingness to show up. When we combine those strengths, we can turn individual acts of generosity into collective impact.

Leadership & Community

Local Leaders. Shared Purpose. Collective Impact.

Our Board is made up of local business leaders who believe in the power of giving back, leading by example, and inspiring other women-owned brands and business owners to take action in their own communities.

These women bring together different industries, experiences, relationships, and talents — but share one belief: business can be a force for good. We encourage women-owned brands and businesses to support one another, open doors for one another, and use what they do best to uplift people and organizations in need.

Whether it's lending expertise, making an introduction, donating a product or service, volunteering time, sponsoring an initiative, amplifying a cause, or simply showing up, our collective strengths make us stronger together.
